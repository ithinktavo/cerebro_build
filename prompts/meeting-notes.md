# Prompt: meeting notes

Turns raw notes, a pasted transcript, or a chat thread into a filed meeting note with decisions and actions.

---

Turn these raw notes into a meeting note for [project or team]: [paste].

1. Write a dated `type: meeting` note in that folder with: Attendees (names and roles only), Context in two lines, Discussion as short bullets in my words, Decisions, and Actions as checkboxes with an owner and a date.
2. Each decision also gets its own dated `type: decision` note (Context, Decision, Alternatives, Consequences), linked from the meeting note and from the hub, unless I say it is minor.
3. Add the actions to the hub's Next steps or Waiting on. Show me the hub change and wait for my yes.
4. Flag anything in the notes that should not be in the vault (personal matters, credentials, customer data) and leave it out.
5. Show me the meeting note in chat before you save it.
