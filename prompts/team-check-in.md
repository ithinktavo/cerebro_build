# Prompt: log a team check-in

For managers and team leads. Use it after a standup or a sync, with the raw notes pasted or dictated. From a 1:1, log only what concerns the team's work; the 1:1 itself gets its own meeting note.

---

Log a check-in for [team]: [paste or dictate the raw notes].

1. Add a dated entry to the team's check-in log with what I said, in my words, cleaned up: items that moved, blockers, who is waiting on what, risks, decisions. Mark the source as "said in chat" with today's date.
2. Update the team hub: Current priorities if they changed, Waiting on (who, what, since when), Risks. Show me the before and after for the hub and wait for my yes.
3. If a line should become a tracker item, say which, and show me the command to create it. Do not run it unless I say yes.
4. List what I promised and to whom, so I can add it to today's daily note.
5. Leave out anything about performance, pay, or personal matters, and tell me you did.
