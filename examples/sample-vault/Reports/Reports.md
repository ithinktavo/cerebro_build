## Recipes

### Weekly status, every Friday

- Audience: the Director of Platform. Where it goes: Alex posts it in Teams. Leadership reads by team. Source: profile, 2026-11-05.
- One status report per team, `YYYY-MM-DD <Team> Status Report.md`, saved in the team folder and listed in its hub: [[Team Alpha]] and [[Team Beta]]. Then one roll-up here, grouped by team. Alex posts the roll-up; design default accepted 2026-11-05.
- Say "Weekly status for all my teams", then "Roll up this week for leadership", or "Close the week" to run both with the shipped list and the weekly review.
- Each report has the same five headings: Done, In progress, Blocked or at risk, Decisions needed, Next. Every line ends with its source. A paste-ready copy comes with each draft, for Teams, where Microsoft 365 Copilot can fit the house format.

### Weekly roll-up

- `YYYY-MM-DD Weekly Roll-Up.md` in this folder, from this week's team reports, every hub's Risks and Open questions, this recipe, and last week's roll-up. Grouped by team. The traffic state is a proposal until Alex confirms it.
- Declined when a team status is missing; the AI offers to draft it first. Each run refreshes Open asks of leadership below.

### Monthly review

- "Monthly review for [month]" writes `YYYY-MM-DD Monthly Review.md` in this folder: outcomes against plan from the hubs' Milestones, decisions made with their status, risks opened and closed, priorities that changed, next month, asks.
